# Slide: Customer Business Value - Jira Analytics on Databricks

## From Jira Data to Measurable Delivery Improvement

**What customers experience**
- **Faster decisions:** Leadership gets a single view of delivery health across projects, sprints, and teams.
- **Earlier risk detection:** Bottlenecks in status transitions, aging issues, and sprint slippage are visible before they become misses.
- **Higher team productivity:** Teams spend less time manually building reports and more time improving execution.
- **Trusted self-service:** Product, engineering, and PMO stakeholders use the same governed metrics in dashboard and Genie Q&A.

**Expected business outcomes (typical targets customers track)**
- **20-40% reduction** in manual Jira reporting effort.
- **15-30% faster** cycle-time diagnosis and remediation.
- **10-20% improvement** in sprint predictability (commit vs complete).
- **1 source of truth** for issue flow, throughput, and worklog analytics.

**Why this project delivers that value**
- Automated bronze -> silver -> gold pipelines reduce manual data wrangling.
- Gold marts standardize KPI definitions (cycle time, transitions, velocity, throughput).
- Lakeview dashboard operationalizes insights for leaders and delivery teams.
- Genie enables non-SQL stakeholders to ask questions in natural language.
